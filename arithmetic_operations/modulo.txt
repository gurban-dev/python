Exercise 1
1 % 3 = 1

What can be subtracted from 1, so that it can be
divisible by 3?

Divisible meaning that there isn't a remainder
after dividing.

1 can be subtracted from 1 to get zero.
And zero is divisible by any number.

1 is congruent to 1 mod 3.

1 - 1 = 0

0 % 3 = 0

Exercise 2
-99 % 100 = 99

99 is congruent to 99 mod 100.

What can you subtract from -99 to make it
divisible by 100?

Answer: 1

-99 - 1 = -100

-100 % 100 = 0

Subtract 99 from 99, so that zero can be divisible
by 100.

Exercise 3
101 % 100 = 1

Subtract 1 from 101, so that 100 can be divisible
by 100.

101 is congruent to 1 mod 100.

101 - 1 = 100

100 % 100 = 0
